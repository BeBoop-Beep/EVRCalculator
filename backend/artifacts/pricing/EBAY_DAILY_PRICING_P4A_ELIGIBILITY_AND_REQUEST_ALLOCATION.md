# P4A: English/NM evidence and adaptive Browse allocation

Market date: 2026-09-19. Branch: `develop`. This is a development candidate, not a language classifier or production certification.

## Request allocation

P1's 150 targets × two queries × three possible pages produced a 900-call **worst-case** search plan. The P3 two-card smoke used four calls for 400 search hits, so six search calls per target were unnecessary for initial candidate supply. The [adaptive capture](../../scripts/run_ebay_p4a_adaptive_development.py) uses one page of the primary query, a second one-page query only if fewer than five local candidates survive, and at most eight getItem calls per target. It stops when three distinct eligible sellers are found. D3-v5 HIGH_CONFIDENCE, fixed-price format, usable USD item price, duplicate item ID, seller diversity, known shipping, and distance from the existing TCGplayer price drive candidate selection; it does not select by cheapest listing alone. The initial 30-target nominal maximum is 60 searches + 240 details = 300 calls, with a 400-attempt per-run hard cap including retries. The targeted [depth extension](../../scripts/run_ebay_p4a_depth_extension.py) has a separate 80-call cap and stops at five sellers. Future P2/P4 calls also share an atomic SQLite UTC-day ledger capped at 1,000 physical Browse attempts. The completed 195-call development run predates that ledger and was not backfilled; older independent research scripts do not use it.

The 30-card cohort spans 15 eras: 14 missing TCGplayer prices, six low-price, five mid-price, and five high-price cards. Sixteen have a resolved variant ID. It includes vintage and modern eras. Initial capture: **42 search calls**, 2,328 raw search hits, 537 local candidates, **119 getItem calls**, 161 total successful calls, zero retries/failures. The depth extension used 34 more getItem calls. Combined development spend: **195 Browse calls**, below both run caps and the 1,000-call application limit. Full provider search and item-detail JSON is retained in local run artifacts; normalized decisions are separate JSONL artifacts. A realistic one-pass expectation from this cohort is about 5.4 calls/card; the initial maximum is 10 calls/card before retries.

## Historical English-safety diagnostic

The [diagnostic artifact](ebay_p4a_historical_english_safety.json) joins the four retained E2.x human-reviewed cohorts to previously sealed text/image/provider/OCR predictions. It is **post hoc and non-certifying**. Candidate positive evidence is IMAGE-v2 MATCH **or** explicit provider Language=English, with D3-v5 HIGH_CONFIDENCE required. IMAGE-v2 MISMATCH, OCR-v3 Japanese mismatch, and any explicit normalized provider non-English value veto. No title, seller location, or marketplace inference is a positive language signal.

## getItem availability and final evidence contract

Across 153 successful item-detail responses, 136 had normalized provider English, one French, one Japanese, and 15 no recognized Language aspect. All 153 exposed item price, image URL, seller feedback score, and buying options; 130 exposed a usable USD shipping cost. The two explicit non-English aspects were excluded. Provider location was never used as language evidence.

Actual `Card Condition` descriptor values included **Near mint or better** (70), **Lightly played (Excellent)** (39), **Moderately played (Very good)** (23), and **Heavily played (Poor)** (19); two detail rows had no descriptor. `condition=Ungraded` alone is unresolved, never Near Mint. The positive NM-compatible mapping is explicit `Near mint or better`/`Near Mint or Better` (or exact Near Mint/Mint variants supported by policy). The played descriptors, damaged and graded/slab cues are excluded. `FIXED_PRICE` must be present and `AUCTION` absent; a mixed auction/BIN row is excluded until an unambiguous executable BIN amount is available. Missing shipping leaves landed ask null and cannot enter the estimator. `BEST_OFFER` alongside `FIXED_PRICE` is allowed because the displayed fixed ask is executable.

Terminal states are `ENGLISH_PRICE_ELIGIBLE`, `NON_ENGLISH_EXCLUDED`, `LANGUAGE_UNRESOLVED`, `IDENTITY_REJECTED`, `CONDITION_UNRESOLVED`, `CONDITION_EXCLUDED`, and `BUYING_FORMAT_EXCLUDED`, each with a reason. A listing enters estimator input only with exact identity, positive English evidence, explicit NM-compatible condition, fixed ask, USD landed price, and unique item identity. The 153 hydrated rows yielded 58 eligible asks, 72 condition exclusions, 15 language unresolved, six unusable fixed landed asks, and two explicit non-English exclusions. Eighteen cards had at least one eligible ask; ten reached three sellers, and eight reached five after the depth extension. These normalized decisions are durable local artifacts. They were **not** copied into P3 Postgres evidence rows because the P4 capture is an independent development run and its already-completed item-detail artifact lacks a per-row capture timestamp required by the P3 table. The collector now records that timestamp for future captures; a validated DB ingestion path remains future work.
