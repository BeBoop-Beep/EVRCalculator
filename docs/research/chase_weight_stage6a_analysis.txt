=== PHASE 18 - behavioral acceptance criteria ===
  Derived from the stated product philosophy BEFORE looking at which
  weight passes. Financial primary, Collector secondary, Chase tertiary.

  C1 CLEAR-FINANCIAL OVERRIDE (binding)
     Chase must not reverse a pair whose Financial gap is >= 10 points
     and whose Financial ordering CONTROL already agrees with.
     Target: rate 0, and the largest Financial gap Chase can overturn
     must stay below 10 with margin under +/-10% price shocks.

  C2 CLOSE-PAIR INFLUENCE (binding)
     Chase must settle a material share of near-ties. Target: it
     reorders at least 10% of pairs whose Financial gap is <= 2 points.
     Below that it is decoration.

  C3 NOT CO-PRIMARY (binding)
     Financial's variance share must stay above 0.80 and Chase's below
     0.20 on every attribution method.

  C4 RANK CONTINUITY (advisory)
     Spearman vs CONTROL >= 0.98 and Top-5 turnover <= 1, so the
     published leaderboard is recognisably the same product.

  C5 WITHIN-SET DIFFERENTIATION (advisory)
     Chase should reorder SOME same-set pairs, since it is the only
     pillar that can - Collector Appeal is set-level and constant there.

  NOTE ON THE INTENDED HIERARCHY. 'Financial >> Collector > Chase'
  cannot be satisfied in variance terms at ANY non-zero Chase weight,
  because Collector Appeal's own variance share in the CURRENT
  production model is approximately zero (see Phase 5). That is a
  property of Collector, not a fault of Chase, and it means the
  hierarchy must be judged behaviourally (C1/C2) rather than by
  variance share.
