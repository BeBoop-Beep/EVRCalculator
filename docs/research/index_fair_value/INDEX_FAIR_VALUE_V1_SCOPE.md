# inDex Fair Value — V1 scope

V1 is English, raw, Near Mint, USD, exact individual canonical cards represented by one explicitly selected physical variant and a trustworthy current TCGPlayer market price. It is a modern research model: the strict live intersection has 4,349 cards in 22 root sets across only Scarlet & Violet and Mega Evolution. Vintage is deferred because exact pull coverage and surviving-NM/condition supply constructs do not support the same estimand.

| Exclusion | Live count over 20,651 canonical cards | Reason | Future support |
|---|---:|---|---|
| No exact accepted pull scarcity | 16,246 | Required acquisition-scarcity input absent | Plausible with new frozen run authority; vintage packs may remain structurally different |
| Collector V7 unavailable | 2,358 | Frozen demand input absent/unscored | Plausible through governed Collector refresh |
| Treatment unresolved | 1,922 | Missing/ambiguous structural presentation metadata | Plausible through taxonomy/identity metadata, not preference fitting |
| No canonical current price | 795 | No positive selected Near Mint USD target | Plausible after identity-safe observations |
| Inadequate/ambiguous identity bridge | 678 | Cannot guarantee target belongs to modeled physical card | Plausible through canonical repair |

Counts overlap. Row-level reasons are retained in the dataset artifact.

Graded cards are excluded: grade, grader, population, gem rate, and premium are a different product and no canonical authority exists. LP/MP/HP/damaged cards are excluded because the target condition is Near Mint and condition effects are not normalized. Non-English cards are outside the canonical English universe. Ambiguous variants, promos without exact physical identity, structurally incompatible observations, and unpriced cards are excluded rather than imputed.

Unsupported cards remain explicit excluded rows and may later enter a separately labeled low-confidence model only after its own preregistration. They are never assigned fake scarcity or zero demand.
