=== PHASE 27 - interpretability ===
  Target language:
    Financial RIP    - How favorable are the complete money outcomes?
    Collector Appeal - How compelling is the card roster?
    Chase            - How rich and attainable is the hunt for
                       economically meaningful hits?
  A pillar that instead means 'another representation of card value' or
  'this box is bigger' is rejected.

  A_100
    reads as: How often does ONE UNIT of this product contain a chase?
    verdict : fails: the answer is dominated by how many packs the unit holds, so the sentence a reader hears is 'bigger boxes are better'

  C_100
    reads as: How many economically meaningful chases does this product actually have?
